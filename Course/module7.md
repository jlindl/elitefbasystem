## Lesson 7.1 - How Amazon's Algorithm Actually Works

**12 min · Lesson 1 of 5**

### The two questions Amazon asks

When someone types a search, Amazon asks two questions:

1. **Is this product relevant?**
2. **Will this product convert (i.e. earn Amazon money)?**

> *"You must tell Amazon: 'This is what my product is relevant for.'"*

That's the whole game. Relevance + conversion.

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: paste your full algorithm explanation.]

### Relevance signals

- **Title keywords** - biggest weight
- **Bullet point keywords** - moderate weight
- **Backend keywords** - for things you can't put in the public copy
- **Product attributes** (size, colour, material) - category-specific weight
- **PPC click-throughs** - implicit relevance signal

### Conversion signals

- **Click-through rate** (CTR) on search results
- **Conversion rate** on the product page
- **Sales velocity** (sales per day vs. competitors)
- **Reviews** - count and rating
- **Price competitiveness**

## Lesson 7.3 - PPC Without Burning Money

**18 min · Lesson 3 of 5**

### The PPC mindset

PPC isn't a "set it and forget it" channel. It's a slow optimisation game.

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: paste your full PPC philosophy.]

### The 3 campaign types

| Type | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto** | Amazon picks keywords for you | Always, week 1 - for keyword discovery |
| **Manual - Broad** | Your keywords, loose match | Once you have data |
| **Manual - Exact** | Your keywords, exact match only | For your top 5–10 winners |

### The PoundMart PPC structure

We run **3 campaigns from launch:**

1. **Auto campaign** - wide net, low bids, harvests new keywords
2. **Manual broad campaign** - your researched keywords, medium bids
3. **Manual exact campaign** - winners only, higher bids

### The weekly review ritual

> *"Review ACOS weekly - lower bids on bad keywords."*

Every Monday, 30 minutes:

1. Pull the **Search Term Report** from Seller Central
2. Add high-converting search terms to your **manual exact** campaign
3. Add bad-converting search terms to your **negative keywords** list
4. Lower bids on keywords above your target ACOS
5. Raise bids on keywords below target ACOS with low impressions

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: detailed walkthrough of the weekly review.]

### Target ACOS

Your target ACOS depends on your margin. The simple rule:

```
Target ACOS = (Profit Margin %) ÷ 2
```

If your product has a 40% profit margin, your target ACOS is around 20%.

## Lesson 7.5 - Vine Reviews

**12 min · Lesson 5 of 5**

### What Vine is

Amazon Vine is Amazon's official review programme. You give away free products to Vine reviewers (vetted by Amazon), and they leave honest reviews - positive or negative.

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: paste your full Vine section.]

### The cost

- **£170** to enrol a product (one-time fee per ASIN)
- **Up to 30 free units** given away to Vine reviewers
- **No control over what they say** - that's the whole point

### When to use Vine

- After your product is live and stable
- When you have <30 reviews and need credibility
- When you have margin to absorb the giveaway cost

### When NOT to use Vine

- Before your listing is fully optimised
- If your product is fundamentally flawed (Vine reviewers will say so)
- Right before a big PPC push (let reviews land first)

> ⚠️ **Watch out**
>
> [The Vine trap - paying £170 + giving away 30 units, getting 3 stars, and tanking your conversion rate.]
