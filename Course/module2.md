**The goal:**

To start selling without paying monthly fees or committing to a Limited Company before your idea has proven itself.

### ✅ To do list

Most “FBA gurus” tell you to set up a limited company, get a trademark, and pay £30+ per month for a professional Amazon plan **before you’ve even sold a product**.

That’s the opposite of what we’re doing here.

We’re keeping it **lean**, because:

1. 🧾 **No monthly subscription** – Amazon only charges £0.75 per sale on the Individual plan.
2. 🧠 **Faster learning** – you can test listing creation, packaging, and FBA shipments right away.
3. ⚙️ **Less admin** – no company accounts, taxes, or trademark paperwork yet.
4. 💸 **Low risk** – if your first idea flops, you can move on with almost no loss.

### Step-by-step:

**Go to:**

 ****https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/

1. **Click** “Sign up” → Choose **Individual Plan**
    
    > It’ll clearly say “No monthly subscription fee”.
    > 
2. **Sign in** with your normal Amazon account (or create a new one for your business).
3. **Enter personal details:**
    - Legal name
    - Address
    - Phone number for verification
4. **Identity verification:**
    
    Upload a photo of your ID and bank statement.
    
    Amazon usually approves within 24–48 hours.
    
5. **Add a deposit method (bank account)**
    
    → this is where Amazon will pay your sales.
    
6. **Add a charge method (card)**
    
    → used for small fees like shipping labels.
    
7. **Confirm tax interview (UK sellers select Individual/sole trader).**

Once that’s done, you’ll land inside **Seller Central** - your new control panel.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **Starting as a Limited Company too early**
    
    → You’ll overcomplicate tax, banking, and setup before earning £1.
    
2. ❌ **Choosing the wrong plan**
    
    → Don’t click “Professional” unless you’re 100% ready to scale.
    
3. ❌ **Ignoring account verification emails**
    
    → Amazon will pause your account if you miss one.
    
4. ❌ **Rushing to list a product**
    
    → Take time to learn the GTIN exemption process (coming in Module 4).
    

## A Quick Tour of Seller Central

You don’t need to learn everything yet - but know these key areas:

| Section | What it’s for |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog → Add a Product** | Where you create or list products |
| **Inventory → Manage FBA Inventory** | Tracks your active listings and stock |
| **Reports → Payments** | Shows your payouts |
| **Performance → Account Health** | Keeps you compliant (very important) |
