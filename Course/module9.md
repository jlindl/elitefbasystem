### The 3 metrics Amazon watches

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Order Defect Rate (ODR) | < 1% |
| Late Shipment Rate (FBM only) | < 4% |
| Cancellation Rate | < 2.5% |

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: explain each metric and how it gets calculated.]

### Common suspension triggers

1. **Inauthentic claims** - a customer says your product is "not authentic"
2. **IP / trademark complaints** - a brand you don't own files against your listing
3. **Safety complaints** - a customer reports a safety issue
4. **Sudden sales spike** - Amazon flags it as suspicious
5. **Multiple accounts** - operating two seller accounts without permission

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: walk through each trigger with specific examples and prevention.]

### If you get suspended

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: paste the suspension response process.]

1. **Don't panic.** Most suspensions are reversible if handled correctly.
2. **Read the suspension notice carefully.** It tells you exactly what they want.
3. **Write a Plan of Action (POA).** Three sections: root cause, immediate actions, preventative actions.
4. **Submit through Seller Central.** Wait 2–7 days for review.
5. **Message Jakub.** Don't wing it on a high-stakes appeal.

- [ ] You have a clear product idea, not just an itch to "do something"

### Should #2 be similar or different?

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: explain the strategic choice - same niche, complementary product, or completely different category.]

| Strategy | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Same niche / category | Brand cohesion, easier marketing | Concentrated risk |
| Complementary product | Bundle opportunities, cross-sells | Slightly more sourcing work |
| New category | Diversified risk | Brand dilution, learning curve |
