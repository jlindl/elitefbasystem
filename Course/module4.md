## Lesson 4.1 - The Anatomy of a Great Listing

**10 min · Lesson 1 of 5**

### What a customer actually sees

When someone lands on your product page, they read in this order:

1. **The main image** (3 seconds of attention)
2. **The title** (5 seconds of skimming)
3. **The price**
4. **The bullets** (if you've earned the click)
5. **The other images** (if they're still interested)
6. **The description** (rarely, but it matters for SEO)

### The 7 elements you control

| Element | Purpose | What it earns you |
|---|---|---|
| Main image | Stop the scroll | The click |
| Title | Confirm relevance | The 5-second read |
| Bullets | Sell the benefits | The "add to cart" click |
| Description | Reinforce + SEO | Repeat customers, search ranking |
| Lifestyle images | Show the use-case | The emotional buy |
| Infographic image | Communicate features | The rational buy |
| Reviews | Earn trust | Conversion at scale |

> 💡 **Jakub's note**
>
> [The order matters more than the words. Most beginners write bullets first because they're "easy" - and end up with a listing that looks like a CV instead of a sales page.]

### Title rules

- **Front-load the keyword.** First 80 characters do the heavy lifting on mobile.
- **Don't keyword-stuff.** Amazon now penalises this.
- **Use plain language.** "Stainless Steel" not "S/Steel".
- **Numbers help.** "Set of 6" beats "multiple".
- **No emojis.** No exclamation marks. No ALL CAPS.

## Lesson 4.3 - Writing Your Bullet Points

**12 min · Lesson 3 of 5**

### What good bullets actually do

Bullets are the second thing a buyer reads. They make or break the conversion.

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: paste your full bullet point section.]

### The PoundMart bullet template

Each bullet follows the same shape:

```
[BENEFIT IN ALL CAPS] – [supporting feature in normal case, plain English]
```

Example:

```
GENTLE ON SENSITIVE SKIN – Made with hypoallergenic cotton, tested on no one but us. Safe for daily use.
```

### The 5 bullets you need

1. **The headline benefit** - the thing that makes them buy
2. **The functional feature** - the spec that backs the benefit
3. **The use-case** - when, where, who it's for
4. **The trust signal** - material, origin, guarantee
5. **The reassurance** - returns, support, satisfaction

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: expand each bullet position with a real example.]

> 💡 **Jakub's note**
>
> [The bullet point trap - writing what *you* think matters, not what the buyer scans for. The fix: read 10 of your competitors' bullets out loud and notice which ones make you actually want to buy.]

### Tools

- **Plain pen and paper** for the first draft. Seriously.
- **[SellerApp Description Editor](https://www.sellerapp.com/amazon-product-description-editor.html)** to clean up formatting before you paste into Seller Central.
