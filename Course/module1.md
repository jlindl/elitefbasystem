## FBA Course Introduction Script

**Opening**

“Welcome.

If you’re watching this, you’ve already taken the first step into building a real Amazon FBA business.

This isn’t a theory course or guru playbook, but a system built from actually selling on Amazon and learning what works the hard way.

It comes from trial and error. From launching products that didn’t work, spending money where I shouldn’t have, making mistakes with stock, pricing, listings, and timing.

I’ve thrown money in the mud so you don’t have to.

Every step in this course exists because something went wrong at some point, and I had to fix it. The goal here is to shortcut that learning curve, avoid the common traps, and help you make smarter decisions from day one.

---

**What FBA Actually Is**

“Amazon FBA stands for Fulfilled by Amazon.

In simple terms, you send your products to Amazon’s warehouses, and they handle storage, shipping, customer service, and returns.

But here’s the important part.

FBA is not a business model by itself. It’s infrastructure.

The business is everything else. Product selection, positioning, pricing, compliance, and scaling. That’s what this course focuses on.”

---

**What This Course Is and Isn’t**

“This is not a get-rich-quick course.

You won’t find screenshots, Lamborghinis, or overnight success stories here.

What you will find is a practical, low-risk way to start selling on Amazon without burning cash or guessing.”

---

**The Core Method**

“The method I teach is the exact path I used.

We start lean as an individual seller.

We validate products before committing serious money.

We use real-world shelf research, not just software.

We create unique bundles to avoid direct competition.

We launch under a Generic brand to test demand properly.

Only once the numbers make sense do we scale.

That’s when we move into a limited company, trademarks, Brand Registry, and real brand building.”

---

**What You’ll Learn Inside**

“Inside the course, you’ll learn:

How to start correctly as an individual seller

How to find product opportunities in physical shops

How to spot demand, gaps, and poor listings

How to create compliant bundles and get GTIN exemptions

How to launch your first ASIN properly

How to read performance data without overreacting

How to know when a product is worth scaling

And how to turn proof of concept into a protected brand”

---

**How to Use This Course**

“This course is designed to be followed in order.

Don’t skip ahead. Don’t rush to launch.

Each module builds on the previous one, and every step is there to reduce risk and protect your account.”

---

**Mindset and Expectations**

“Progress beats perfection.

Your first product doesn’t need to be special. It needs to work.

Most people fail because they overthink or overinvest too early.

This system is about doing the opposite. Prove demand first, then scale.”

---

**Closing**

“If you follow the process, stay compliant, and focus on execution, this course can take you from zero to your first profitable FBA product the right way.

Let’s get started.”
