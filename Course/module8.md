# Module 8 - Expansion

> **What you'll walk away with:** A clear plan for moving beyond Amazon - into eBay, TikTok Shop, your own website, or international Amazon marketplaces. Plus the discipline to know which to pick first (and which to skip).

> _Note from Jakub:_ Most sellers expand too early. They've got one half-working product on Amazon and they want to be on five platforms. Don't be that. Expansion is for products that are already winning.

---

## When you're ready for this module

> *"Once you've validated your product, built your brand, and created a stable Amazon listing, it's time to grow beyond a single platform."*

You should only be in Module 8 when:

- [ ] You have a product with consistent weekly sales on Amazon
- [ ] You have at least 25–50 reviews
- [ ] Your PPC is profitable (ACOS at or below target)
- [ ] You have inventory cashflow to support a second channel

If any of these are missing, **stay in Modules 6 and 7.**

## Lesson 8.1 - Choosing Your Next Channel

**12 min · Lesson 1 of 4**

### The expansion question

You can expand in three directions:

1. **More products on the same channel** (Amazon UK)
2. **Same product on more channels** (eBay, TikTok, Shopify)
3. **Same product on more Amazon marketplaces** (Amazon EU, US)

### The trade-offs at a glance

| Channel | Effort to launch | Cashflow needs | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| eBay | Low | Low | Bargain hunters, niche collectors |
| TikTok Shop | Medium | Medium | Impulse buyers, viral-driven |
| Own Shopify | High | Medium-high | Direct, brand-loyal |
| Amazon US | High | High | Massive but competitive |
| Amazon EU | Medium | Medium | Multi-language, VAT complex |

### The PoundMart expansion order

For most students, in this order:

1. **eBay** - simplest, fastest, validates your product on a second channel
2. **TikTok Shop** - leverage organic content if your product is visual
3. **Shopify** - only when you have a brand worth direct-selling
4. **Amazon US/EU** - only when you've maxed Amazon UK

Before you move to Module 9, you should have:
