# Module 6 - Launching a Brand

> **What you'll walk away with:** A registered Ltd company, a filed trademark, and an Amazon Brand Registry application - ready to unlock A+ Content, Vine, and brand-protected listings.

> _Note from Jakub:_ This is the gear-shift. Up until now we've been a reseller bundling products. From this module on, we're a brand. **You only do this when your product is already selling.**

---

## When to start this module

You should only be in Module 6 when **all** of these are true:

- [ ] You've had repeat orders or stable weekly sales
- [ ] You've validated your bundle/product works
- [ ] You have profit to reinvest
- [ ] You're ready to commit (this involves real money - Ltd, trademark, design)

If you're not there yet, **stay in Modules 3–5 and keep selling.** This module isn't going anywhere.

## Lesson 6.3 - Naming, Logo, and Brand Identity

**12 min · Lesson 3 of 5**

### Naming rules (boring but critical)

A name has to:

- [ ] Be **available** as a trademark in your category
- [ ] Be **available** as a Companies House registration
- [ ] Be **available** as a `.com` or `.co.uk` domain (ideally both)
- [ ] Be **searchable** - not generic, not too clever
- [ ] Be **pronounceable** - the customer needs to remember it

[CONTENT FROM NOTION: paste your full naming framework.]

### How to check availability

1. **Companies House** - [find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/)
2. **UK Trademark search** - [www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark)
3. **Domain search** - Namecheap, GoDaddy, etc.
4. **Amazon search** - make sure no competing brand is already on Amazon under a similar name

### The logo (lean version)

You don't need a £5,000 logo. You need a clean wordmark.
