# Module 5 — Sending to Amazon

> **What you'll walk away with:** Your first inventory shipped to Amazon's FBA warehouse, correctly labelled, packed, and live for sale.

> _Note from Jakub:_ This module is procedural. There's a right way and a wrong way, and the wrong way costs you time and money. Read every step.

---

## What FBA actually means here

> *"FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon) means Amazon stores, picks, packs, and ships your product for you."*

But only if you ship it to them correctly. That's what this module is about.

Every box gets a box label printed from your shipment plan. It contains the destination warehouse code and a barcode that links the box to your shipment.

### After it ships

- **Track the shipment** in Seller Central → Manage FBA Shipments.
- **Wait for "Checked-in" status.** This is when Amazon physically scans your boxes at the warehouse.
- **Wait for "Receiving" → "Closed".** Inventory only goes live for sale once it's fully checked in. This can take 3–10 days.

> 💡 **Jakub's note**
>
> [The waiting period is the worst. It's normal. Use the time to start drafting your launch plan for Module 6.]

### Module checklist

Before you move to Module 6, you should have:

- [ ] A shipment plan created and submitted in Seller Central
- [ ] Every unit FNSKU-labelled (or paid Amazon to label)
- [ ] Boxes within size and weight limits
- [ ] Box labels printed and applied correctly
- [ ] Carrier booked (Amazon Partnered or own)
- [ ] Tracking visible in your shipment dashboard

When inventory is checked-in and live for sale, your **first sales window opens.** Welcome to the game.
